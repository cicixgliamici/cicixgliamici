### Paper Implementations & Formalizations

Projects that begin with reading a technical paper and translating its ideas into executable code, formal definitions, proofs, or engineering constraints. The underlying ideas are attributed to their authors; implementation scope and extensions are described in the repositories.

| Project | Paper or technical foundation | Implementation focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Abstracting Abstract Machines](https://github.com/cicixgliamici/abstracting-abstract-machines) | *Abstracting Abstract Machines* — David Van Horn and Matthew Might | Concrete and finite-address abstract machines in OCaml, garbage collection, reference semantics, and experiments. |
| [A Weak Silly Substitution Calculus](https://github.com/cicixgliamici/a-weak-Silly-substitution-Calculus) | *Mirroring Call-by-Need, or Values Acting Silly* — Beniamino Accattoli and Adrienne Lancelot | Lean 4 formalization of a growing fragment, including labelled reductions, the Call-by-Silly strategy, and a weak-normal-form characterization. |
| [SimHash Logs](https://github.com/cicixgliamici/simhash-logs) | Charikar's similarity estimation and Manku, Jain, and Das Sarma's near-duplicate detection | Log normalization, SimHash fingerprints, candidate indexes, and comparison against brute-force matching in Go. |
| [RFR-HJM-FMM](https://github.com/cicixgliamici/rfr-hjm-fmm) | *Alternative Risk-Free Rates and the New Indexation Framework: An Analysis of Compounded Overnight Rates with Applications to FRNs and Swaps* | Deterministic pricing and risk calculations in Python, with FMM/HJM scaffolds; coupled stochastic pricing remains planned. |
| [NASA Power of Ten Demo](https://github.com/cicixgliamici/nasa-power-of-ten-demo) | NASA/JPL's *The Power of Ten: Rules for Developing Safety-Critical Code* | An engineering study applying the paper's rules to a small C ring buffer, with tests, contracts, and rule-to-code traceability. |
